Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

This policy outlines what data Key System Bot and Cys Config Hub store and how it is used.

1. What We Store

  • Discord Profile: Your Discord User ID, username, and avatar URL to link your uploaded configs and role access.
  • Config Files: Configuration files, names, and tags you choose to upload publicly.
  • License Keys: Key redemption status and authorized server IDs for the verification bot.

2. How We Use It

  • To verify your role in Discord and authenticate your login.
  • To display your username as the creator on configs you upload.
  • To prevent spam and block malicious uploads.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

  • Data is stored securely on Cloudflare's global infrastructure. We never sell or share user data.
  • You can delete your uploaded configs at any time directly through the Config Hub.
  • For full account data removal, contact us via our Discord Server.

4. Contact

Questions? Reach out on our official Discord Server.
